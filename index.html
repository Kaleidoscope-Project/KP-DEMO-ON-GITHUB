Kaleidoscope Project, Modeling Technique, and Software Applications

The Kaleidoscope Project is a private R&D initiative intended to expand the classical dynamical systems concept by introducing these systems' qualitative perception paradigm and to explore the feasibility of developing qualitative systems mathematical modeling technique enabling creating qualitative models in the form of a system of algebraic expressions or as Multicolored Logical Net (McLN).

This website serves as the presentation hub for modeling technique solutions, and of project’s software applications supporting the models development. They are detailed on the dedicated webpages. Below is the annotated list of links to all these pages.

Solution 1: Modeling Single Variable States Dependence
on the States of Groups of Other Variables

This page is titled: "AoS-Based Extended Disjunctive Normal Form Expression Calculator". It presents and computes several interactive examples of your choice, illustrating how the states of a single qualitative variable depend on the states of groups of other qualitative variables. Each example defines the variable dependence as Extended Disjunctive Normal Form (XDNF) written and computed as the composition of operations of the Algebra of Symbols. Open the Page

Solution 2: Modeling Qualitative Dynamical Systems
Behavior Based on the System State Space Approach

This page, titled: "Qualitative Dynamical Systems Vector State-Space Matrix Equation Calculator", presents and computes several interactive examples illustrating how the qualitative dynamical systems can be modeled based on defining their models in the form of matrix state equations. Suggesting to choose a simple predefined model and a step-by-step computation style, this page shows the application of the systems' behavior simulation mechanism implemented as the sequence of state vectors transformations and visualizes the results of each computational step. Open the Page

Solution 3: Modeling Qualitative Dynamical Systems
as the Multicolored Logical Net

This page, titled: "Multicolored Logical Net Modeling Formalism", introduces the qualitative dynamical systems graphical modeling approach designed for developing these systems qualitative models in the form of bipartite oriented graph and reproducing the systems behavior as changes in the colors of graphical elements of the models. Open the Page
