Kaleidoscope Project Applications
Live Demo Presentation

As is mentioned on the other pages, the Kaleidoscope project has two Java desktop applications: "The Qualitative Dynamical Systems Modeling & Simulation Environment" and "The Space Exploration Mission // Agent-Robot Behavior Simulator"

This page provides live presentation of these two applications. It has been made possible thanks to an innovative tool called "CheerpJ", developed by the compony "Leaning Technologies". This technology is open-source and available for free-software development at no cost.

CheerpJ is one of the most successful solutions on the market designed to run Java applications and applets in modern web browsers by compiling Java bytecode into WebAssembly and JavaScript. Leaning Technologies is a British-Dutch company that specializes in compile-to-JavaScript and compile-to-WebAssembly solutions. The CheerpJ is one of the most successful products.

The Demo is running in "Carousel Show" mode organized as demonstration loop presenting several models developed in the QDS MSE application or several exploration tasks executed in the SEM ABS application. During the show, each model or exploration task is displayed on the screen only for a short time before automatically transitioning to the next. All demos run continuously, like a carousel, until the presentation is interrupted by you. Both Apps were developed using browser Google Chrome and tested in Microsoft Edge. Consequently, they are the recommended browsers.

Please Note! The presentation of the Java Desktop Applications in a browser requires compiling the Java applications into a format that modern browsers can render and animate. Therefore, it may take several seconds to bring the application into the browser and complete forming its image before it can be shown on the screen. Please be patient.

More on Opening Swing Apps in a Browser!

Despite the major achievement making it possible to present Java Swing Applications on the web page, the intrinsic communication between hosted applications and the browser is currently limited. Browsers treat the apps as a single HTML element. However, the swing apps have their own inner layout and scaling mechanisms. Therefore, when the browsers perform zooming in or out they make apps appear either effectively larger or smaller. This is because at present days they do not have means to inform the apps that they are undergoing zooming. As a consequence, the application's inner layout may break.

To resolve this issue, follow these steps. First, open the app presentation page, and preview it. Depending on the browser's current zoom level the app may appear either too large or too small. Please, change the browser's zoom to make the size of the app appropriate for your purpose. In the result, the zoomed application's layout may look distorted. To fix this layout issue, reload the app by clicking the browser's "Reload this page" icon located near the address bar. This time the app layout will be generated by taking into account the browser's current zoom level, and you will see the app layout that fits nicely in the center of the page. Feel free to resize your browser window. The app will stay in the page center always. Just do not change the page zoom again.

Please choose a presentation from the links below. The Apps will be shown in the separate tabs of the browser you are using.

The Presentations are started automatically when the Welcome Popup shown in the center of the App is closed. To do this, click anywhere within the popup rectangle. Alternatively, you can press either the ESC button or the SPACE bar on your keyboard. This will close the popup and the show will begin.
